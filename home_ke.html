Welcome to the beauty of Morocco

A beautiful country with a rich history

Located in the northwest of Africa, it has a great location, surrounded by the Atlantic Oceon and the Medediterranean sea. Morocco is famous for good food and the beautiful cities.

Moroccan cities are full of color and history . Marrakech is famous for its busy markets and lively square, Jamaa el-fna.Fez has one of the oldest universities in the world and beautiful old streets. If you love nature and photos Chefchaouen , the blue city is the perfect place for you.

Quick facts:

  • 🏛 Capital: Rabat
  • 🏞 Largest City: Casablanca.
  • 💁‍♀️ Oficcial Languages : Arabic and Amazigh.
  • 💰 Currency: Moroccan Dirham.

Best time to visit Morocco:

  1. Spring (March to May) : Warm days and blooming nature.
  2. Autumn (September to November) : Comfortable temperatures.
  3. Winter (december to February) : Cool weather.
  4. Summer (June to August) : Hot but great for coastal towns like Essaouira.
