Three must-try dishes in Morocco

Couscous

Moroccan couscous typically made from semolina, it can be vegan with seven vegetabales like carrot, turnip, chickpeas, cabbage... All cooks in broth and flavoured with parsley and spices such as turmenic and ginger or can be served with meat with these vegetables.

Tagine

Moroccan tagine is a slow-cooked stew named after the traditional cone-shaped clay pot it is prepared in. it is usually made with slow-cooked meat, vegetable, and spices such as ginger and saffron.

Pastilla

This kind of dish combines crispy pastry stuffed with savory chicken, roasted almonds, and spices. finished with sweet cinnamon and sugar.

Step-by-Step Preparation🫕:

-Couscous:

  1. Saute the meat with onions, tomatoes, oil spices and pour water.
  2. Raw couscous grains with oil
  3. Add vegetables

-Tagine:

  1. Place the meat in the clay with chopped onions, garlic, oils, and spices over low heat.
  2. Put the vegetables over the meat.
  3. Add a small cup of water mixed with spices.
  4. cook over low heat for one to two hours until the meat is well cooked.

-Pastilla:

  1. Cook chicken with onion, herbs, and spices, Then whisk the eggs.
  2. Fry almonds and mix it with cinnamon, powdered sugar and orange blossom water.
